Well hello again. Long time no speak. Has it really been a month and a half since my last post? It's a good job nobody is reading this yet or they would presume the project was abandoned.

Quite, quite the opposite. I've been wrist deep in Blender for a long time now and i'm finally ready to share the progress i've been able to make. And progress I have indeed made...

![Screenshot 1](/images/margincall/mc1p3.png)

Welcome to the first look at the expanded game world for Margin Call! This has been a solid month and a half of work over here to create the groundwork for the world and playspace of Margin Call. Featuring the (Currently named) New Pork Stock Exchange inside a towering city on the little bit of land known (currently) as New Pork Island.

![Screenshot 2](/images/margincall/mc2p3.png)

More city to come later, for now this is part of the set dressing.

Here we can see a little more of the new gamespace players will be financially fleecing each other. The giant chart wall still dominates the room, but we now have these trade clusters at the front of the room. What a shame that this is just a post about the modelling work, i'd love to be able to say more about what they're for. Oh well, Mañana.

![Screenshot 6](/images/margincall/mc6p3.png)

I mean that's all well and good, but the world looks kind of bleak and sterile right? That's because i've just finished my first modelling pass for the gameworld. According to the stats, I have so far created 119 decorative office props with 10 of them rigged with armatures to be posable in the gameworld. This is a huge deal for me in terms of progress, but now I need to go place the items in the world to give it more character. I've also built a nice pipeline for outputting my ridiculous Blender file into Godot in a friendly manner so I can model in one and game in the other. Anecdotally this has been tested and it feels amazing to be running around in the world i've built, but I held off on connecting anything complex until the modelling was at a stable point. More to come on this at some point.
