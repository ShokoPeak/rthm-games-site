What's good, internet? Matt here again with a little update around what's been going on with Margin Call!

With the addition of workbot pushing out the technical details of what's been changing, I admit that i've not made much time to post an update on the non technical aspects of Margin Call but I have been busy in the background. Firstly, I invested in a graphics tablet to help with texturing / sprites and have had some great success using it for the spritework on my gamejam project PlagueNIC, which has doubled as a test game for features I am nowhere near ready to start working on for MC.

So, what's new in Margin Call? Firstly - i've been investing quite a bit of time into improving in Blender and I feel like i'm ready to share. Welcome to the new first map for MC - the JPSE! (working title)

![Screenshot 1](/images/margincall/mc1p2.png)

The first map is based on the New York Stock Exchange building, a landmark of Wall Street and an edifice of capitalism. Yay for money! But unlike the real NYSE, you don't need $10M just to use the bathrooms.

Fun fact - You might be incredibly surprised to learn that the NYSE takes security very seriously, and as such it has been quite difficult to find reference material for the building. Also i'm pretty sure i'm on a watchlist now, so my other project to build a subterrenean digging machine to tunnel my way inside is on indefinite hiatus.

Luckily, the building is featured extensively in the NYSE's socials and there are some truly excellent artistic renderings of the building by people such as Australian architectual artist Simon Fieldhouse to draw inspiration from. (tunneling machine on standby)

![Screenshot 2](/images/margincall/mc2p2.png)

This gives a bigger playspace for the map which will host a number of the trading desks as you might find inside the real building. Will these desks do anything? Do they add new gameplay mechanics? Are they resistant to tunnel boring machines? My answer is an enthusiastic "YES! (probably)" but more details on that in a later update. I do have some cool ideas to allow players to play the market differently...

![Screenshot 3](/images/margincall/mc3p2.png)

We still have our huge market window (with a sick speaker array) which sits quite nicely against the far wall, but the interior is very much WIP. We don't even have a bell yet...

![Screenshot 4](/images/margincall/mc4p2.png)

But we do have more columns than the real building. Take that, Wall Street! Currently this all lives inside Blender and will do for the near future, but hopefully this gives a taste of where the project is heading. This will not be the only map, I have a few ideas and would like to have some similar sized and smaller maps to support different game sizes and maybe even a single player map for chill solo trader runs. All will be revealed as the project develops. I'm excited.

That's it for now folks, so thanks for reading and i'll see you for the next update. Stay tuned!

\- Matt, RthmGames
