First post. What's good, world?

Who is RthmGames?

RthmGames is... me. It's one person right now. Hi. Well technically it's a three person team if you count my two workshy, freeloading cats. I'm a solo developer working on my first ever fully featured game. I don't have any fancy accomplishments to flash, I haven't ever worked in the industry and honestly i'm here chasing a dream.

Since Christmas I have been working on my first ever fully featured game - Margin Call!

Margin Call is a single player / multiplayer game based around one simple goal - trade the market. Easy right? Become a trade on Wall Street? Not in this game. I'm building Margin Call with one clear motto in mind:

![It is not enough to succeed. Others must fail.](/images/it-is-not-enough-to-succeed-others-must-fail-mandy.png)

Because the only thing more satisfying than having money is knowing others don't, right?

![Margin Call First Dev Shots](/images/margincall/MC1firstpost.png)

Margin Call turns the chaos of a trading floor into a party game where you win and lose the kind of money that would make a president blush on the strength of the items you find and the signals you read. You get a full day from 9AM to 5PM to trade your little hearts out, but watch out. Players that lose more than they can pay back pay the price - they get MARGIN CALLED. And as anyone who has ever been Margin Called in real life can tell you, that's no good thing. Make it to 5PM and maybe you see another trading day.

So where is the game at today?

Currently, the game is in a playable state but very sparse. I've been concentrating on building the systems out with design to follow and ultimately what I have today is a tech demo - but a promising one.

![Well. Most of the time.](/images/margincall/MC2firstpost.png)

So right now i'd say that the game is maybe like 20% complete. In terms of artwork there is basically nothing, the item list is very sparse and there's a huge list of features i'm working through. That said, the game already has a pretty nice list of mechanics for players to ruin each others days with:

- Real time market simulation - Seeded market, generates candlestick data and responds to real in game trades. Get rich enough and you become a market maker (pump and dump anyone?)

- Full candlestick chart data plus 4 small charts showing historical data. Fool yourself with confirmation bias!

- ATM system - Smart money knows a 5% interest return is a safe bet. You can deposit cash to generate profit but this casual method does nothing to power up your character. Coasting only.

- Item shop - Because roguelike mechanics. You love them. I love them. Lets move on.

- Social mechanics - a voting system and a donation system that both tie into directing the course of the game for everyone. And for the more underhanded, there's a bribery system and a loan system player to player. You earn interest. They pay interest. You do the math.

- NPC support - currently up to 7 NPCs but this isn't much of a hard cap and I think we would be able to support 15 NPCs and a 16 player lobby. NPCs act like players but with specific personalities that affect their trading style.

- Text chat and emote wheel. The text chat window with built in trade log is heavily inspired by the WoW text window. Available in game and in lobby.

- 3 modes of going "bust" - Bust, Out and MCHC. Bust just sets your cash to zero and you get the slot machine at the end of day intermission which gives you the next days starting cash. Good for beginners / casual lobbies. Out is more punishing - not only do you lose your cash, you are liquidated if you are Margin Called during play. This means your items are sold and used to pay of debtors such as the market debt and anyone you owe loans too. You come back end of day and get the slot machine. You'll need it. MCHC is similar to out but if you're out, you don't come back

- LAN multiplayer support currently working well - max 8 players, full state replication with host authority. Some mild mp progression systems planned but don't expect weapon mods or kill streaks, most of the progress will be SP gated. We have disconnect / reconnect handling and NPC takeover for players gone longer than 30 seconds.

- Progression and identity - Players have three character slots which both track gameplay stats. Characters rank up in SP by collecting "Alpha" at the end of each run, and "Beta" from MP games. Read into that what you will. Character creator will be included but is awaiting art assets. Games have persistance as part of the loop so you're unlocking things as you go.

- Steam integration - currently as far as I can go without registering the game on Steam to begin testing. That's my next big move.

Aaaaand loads of other stuff but that's all for now.
