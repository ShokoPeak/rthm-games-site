The biggest cycle in a while: six sessions, ten commits, and the test suite climbed from 246 to 394. Headline items are a whole new player-feedback platform (smoke plumes, fireworks, camera shake, a 7-rung fail-sound ladder) and a ground-up PlayerHUD rebuild.

**Wall banners live (07-21)**

- First 8 advert artworks are in and covering all 8 slots. But the banner-face UVs shipped **collapsed** in the glb — u constant, zero span on the height axis, confirmed against the buffer rather than the modelling-side audit.
- Rather than block on a re-unwrap, the banner system now **projects art from the cloth quad's model-space position** in a small shader, with bounds auto-measured from the mesh at bind time. Mesh UVs are bypassed entirely, so re-exports self-heal.

**UI scale, round two (07-21/23)**

- **Font softness root-caused:** the tablet's dock/expand is a per-Control scale tween, and Godot 4.5's oversampling rework is per-*viewport* — it doesn't follow CanvasItem transforms, so expanded text was bilinear-stretched soft and docked text was crunchy. Fix: MSDF default font + mipmaps, which makes transform-scaled text scale-independent everywhere including the chart's drawn labels.
- **Per-element scale sliders** — Settings → Graphics now has a collapsible "Advanced Scale" section with one slider per HUD element (trade HUD, order queue, rankings, chat log, tablet docked/expanded), 50–150%, live while dragging. Elements grow *into* the screen from their anchor corner; interactive tablet states clamp to the viewport so element × global scaling can't push buttons off-screen.
- Tablet page footprint now exactly equals the expanded footprint at every scale factor, and corner radius is unified at 10.

**Booth panel style pass, Phase A (07-21)**

- All five casino booth panels moved onto shared theme variations for titles, muted text and error labels, and onto the settings-driven colour helpers — which means **colourblind mode now actually works booth-wide** (every panel had been hardcoding its own green/red).
- EOD panel brought into line with the others (layer, backdrop, hint text) and given an **armed-confirm step**, closing one of the LAN playtest action items.

**Prediction booth rebuilt as a bet slip (07-21)**

- The detail view was "cumbersome for anyone new", so it's a full redesign: question is the hero, live probability + inline sparkline in one row, stake chips, and **two-line BUY buttons quoting the true fill** for your selected stake (routed through the real AMM so rake and depth are included, re-quoted on every change).
- **Dollars only** — shares never surface. Positions read "Your bet: $30 on YES — pays $92 if right", with CASH OUT buttons quoting live sell value.
- The separate confirm step is gone; the quote on the button *is* the confirmation. Clients now mirror cost basis so the dollar language holds in multiplayer.

**New item + booth accounting (07-22/23)**

- **The $N000 Suit** — a rare consumable that intercepts a margin call: the position still force-closes and the loss is real, but instead of going out you're revived with 3× the day's goal in cash. It's **magic money** — the day-start baseline shifts by the injected amount, so the revive never counts toward your goal. Consumed on use, then returns to your pool one tier more expensive, per player.
- **Booth wagers now count as trades** — settle entries feed the EOD board's trade count and win rate, plus the tablet's P&L and history pages, which gained a TYPE column with outcomes.
- Booth boards gained per-booth art loops with staggered ripple transitions.

**Sprint, debug menu, derived throughput (07-26)**

- **Sprint on Shift** — ×1.6 for 2 s, 3 s recharge, 1 s penalty on a full drain, with a gold stamina bar under the nametag.
- **Dev debug menu** ("-", debug builds only): cash and commission bumps plus free item grants from a type-grouped list. Makes testing everything below far faster.
- **Order throughput is now derived** — `$10k × day × multipliers` rather than a static base, recomputed on demand. Also fixes vote-won budget upgrades being wiped by the daily bonus re-apply.
- Item purchases now apply their effects *before* the withdraw, so margin and risk labels update on purchase instead of on your next trade.

**The visual feedback platform (07-27)**

The big one: a coherent visual and audio language for what a player is doing, readable from anywhere in the room and extensible without code changes. Built as an event bus feeding independent renderers, driven off existing synced signals so it needed **zero new RPCs**.

- **A 7-tier magnitude ladder** measured as one event's P&L against the day's goal — so it stays meaningful as goals grow from $600 to $100M. Each tier *adds* an element rather than scaling one, so you recognise a tier by which components are present.
- **Losses throw a smoke plume**: burst, column, shed fog, light, ground ring, and at the top rung a ceiling splash — the plume is capped by the room's actual ceiling, and hitting it *is* the top-tier tell. Plumes spawn in the world, not on the body, so you walk away from your own crater.
- **Wins launch a firework** — shell rises, bursts at apex, glitter and crackle fall under gravity. Structurally distinct from the plume (additive sparks vs mix-blended fog), not just recoloured.
- **Camera shake and screen flash** at the top two rungs, both behind an accessibility intensity slider and a shake toggle.
- **A 7-rung fail-sound ladder** authored by Matt. Sounds deliberately *don't* layer the way visuals do — layering samples is just mud — and audio doesn't consume effect-budget slots, so an event whose plume got evicted is still audible.
- Effects share a strict-outrank budget with eviction; a 12-event storm against 8 slots settles at exactly 8 active.

Four rounds of debugging went into getting the plume to actually reach the ceiling, and the lessons are worth writing down:

- **With additive particles, visible height ≠ travelled height.** The colour ramp faded to black by end of life, and a black additive particle is an invisible one — the column stopped rendering a third of the way up while still physically rising.
- **Visible column length = min(emit window, particle lifetime) × speed.** A short emit window against a long lifetime gives you a rising *band*, not a filled column. The firework's shell deliberately inverts the same rule to make a comet.
- **The real killer was particle forces** — Godot defaults turbulence initial displacement to ±5 m, scattering every particle at birth, compounded by turbulence noise and gravity. Column emitters now build with those zeroed, so height is exactly speed × lifetime and the ceiling clamp means something.
- **GPU particles do not simulate headless** — no rendering device, so capture reads zero at every timestep. Particle *config* can be verified from the CLI; particle *motion* cannot.

**Balance pass, same commit:** default starting cash 10000 → **1000** ("makes day one more of a challenge even if you can read the markets"), the starter margin item 5× → 3×, and per-item leverage moved out of a hardcoded dict onto the item definitions, so retuning margin is content tuning now.

**Camera beat fix (07-27)**

The start-of-day chart-wall reveal was reported as a regression. It wasn't — driving a real boot proved the camera eased to the wall pose exactly as designed. The cause: **any movement input cancelled the beat instantly**, and you start the day by pressing E at a station you've just walked to, so a still-held W killed the shot within one frame. Fixed with a grace window plus a stick deadzone. A matching end-of-day beat was also built — it turned out never to have existed.

**PlayerHUD rebuilt (07-28)**

From Matt's mockups: the HUD went from a tall vertical stack to a short, wide, **size-locked** bottom strip at fixed 880×126 (locked because a 2D background plate is being drawn for it).

- Three columns, each a fixed slot except the trade column, which is the one expanding child — so it *pays* for the position column when a trade opens, by abbreviating rather than by squashing the wings.
- New **day track**: today's P&L drawn as a curve against the $0 / target / bonus lines, replacing the old text goal line. Samples locally, so clients draw it too — no new RPCs.
- **EXIT button** replaces BUY|SELL while holding, tinted by live unrealised P&L.
- **Risk is now a rung ladder** (1/2/3/4% then 5..100%) — at a late-run bankroll a 5% floor is too big to work through the book.
- **Fixed a real launch bug found along the way:** settings applied on the first process frame, which deferred the *audio bus volumes* too — so the menu's title music played its opening frames at full 0 dB, a ~14 dB blast at a 20% setting. Audio now applies immediately; only graphics stays deferred.

**Next:** bank/ATM quick-amount options (workshop first, to keep the panel clean), scrolling booth names on the board colour bands, bootstrapping more of the world's test-pattern screens, and finishing the feedback platform's last phase — win SFX, the accessibility settings UI, and migrating the two legacy effects onto the platform. And a long-owed in-game eyeball pass: the firework has never been seen in motion and the win side is still silent.
