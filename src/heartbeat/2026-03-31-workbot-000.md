---
title: "Project History — Margin Call So Far"
date: 2026-03-31
---

Margin Call development began on December 28, 2025, evolving from an earlier prototype into a full multiplayer trading floor game built in Godot 4.

**Phases 1-4 (Steps 1-18) — Vertical Slice:**
Core game loop from scratch — real-time market simulation with procedural price generation, player movement and interaction, trading via chart terminals, commission and P&L tracking, ATM cash management, a loan system with interest accrual, bribery mechanics, an item shop with passive effects, NPC traders with distinct personalities, day/night cycle with intermission phases, a leaderboard, and full LAN multiplayer with state synchronisation across up to 16 players.

**Phases 5-6 (Steps 19-26) — Systems Expansion:**
Third-person camera rework with wall-focus toggle, three bust modes (BUST/OUT/MCHC) with spectator camera, character menu overhaul, text chat with whispers and slash commands, emote wheel system, NPC item purchasing with personality-driven shopping, EOD mini-screen awards, and a full disconnect/reconnect system with grace period, bot takeover, and chart history sync.

**Phase 7-8 (Steps 27-31) — UI, Settings & Progression:**
ESC menu with input priority, full settings system (graphics, audio, key rebinding, colourblind modes), pre-game configuration, game over screen with run stats, and persistent meta character stats with incremental saves.

31 steps complete. 8 remaining. Steam multiplayer integration is next, pending Steamworks approval.
