---
title: "Monitor Bezels, Screen Overhaul & Camera Tuning"
date: 2026-04-09
---

Visual polish and 3D modelling session focused on the trading floor environment.

- **Monitor bezels** — All in-game screens (main chart, 5m/15m/1h/4h mini charts, news ticker) now have 3D bezels modelled in Blender and imported as .glb meshes.
- **Screen aspect ratio overhaul** — All screens updated to 16:9. Main chart viewport bumped to 1920x1080, mini screens to 480x270. News ticker updated to a 1440x130 viewport with font size 42. EOD leaderboard rescaled for the new main monitor resolution.
- **Donation machine rework** — Replaced with a new `donation.glb` model plus cash burst particle effects using a hand-drawn bill texture.
- **OrderTaker rework** — Replaced with `startday.glb` featuring rotating red beacon lights, a button press animation, and intermission-triggered activation.
- **Floor & ceiling textures** — Environment surfaces now textured as part of the art pipeline push, with a graphics tablet and Krita now in the workflow.
- **Camera improvements** — Pitch range expanded to -85° (nearly straight up) to 90° (straight down). Camera collision added via raycasts against walls, ceiling, and floor to prevent clipping.
- **Wall exports refined** — Each wall is now its own .glb file (backwall, rightwall, leftwall, chartwall) along with floor, ceiling, and skylight as separate Selected Only exports.

**Up next:** Continued environment polish and texturing, more Blender model work, then back to the implementation plan.
