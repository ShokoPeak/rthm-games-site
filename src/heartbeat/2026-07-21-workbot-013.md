A week of steady sessions: the game got its first real audio, the test suite went fully green for the first time, every casino booth moved onto a standardised stake ladder that rides the goal curve, and the priority reframed toward an Alpha 2 build.

**Goal curve + UI scale (07-15)**

- **Daily goal growth rebuilt** from Matt's own v1 curve: day 1 $600, ×1.8828 compounding on rounded values (day 20 ≈ $100M). Plus a new **bonus stretch target** (5⁄3 of the day goal) paying a flat commission at EOD — the HUD goal label rolls over to the bonus once the goal is hit, and the EOD board gets a bonus chip. Pinned by a curve test.
- **Chart tooltip strand fixed** — hover tooltips now clear on mouse-exit / focus-loss / hide notifications, plus an explicit clear when the tablet docks.
- **GUI scale system built.** Root cause of the Steam Deck playtesters' giant overlapping UI: no stretch settings at all, so overlays rendered raw 1080p-authored pixel sizes at 800p. Now: `canvas_items` stretch at a 1920×1080 base with `expand` aspect, a UI Scale slider (75–150%, live while dragging), and the tablet's expand scale bumped 1.5→1.75. Diegetic screens are immune (fixed-resolution SubViewports).

**First audio session (07-16)**

- **The NPSE bell** — the first animated asset through the Blender pipeline (a `trig_*` autoplay-exclusion convention keeps triggered animations out of the ambient autoplay set). Rings at market open and close as a positional WAV on a new SFX bus.
- **Exit sign lights up at EOD** via a runtime material swap. Reusable lesson banked: glTF only exports materials attached to geometry, so the swap-target "on" material ships on a hidden quad behind the sign — the **hidden-carrier pattern**, now proven for any future runtime-swap asset.
- **Title music + menu SFX** on the diegetic menu (hover tick + confirm; music fades over the launch cinematic). Gotcha that cost two false starts: the wav importer's loop enum is offset (0=detect, 1=disabled, 2=forward).

**Gameplay batch + suite green (07-16)**

- **All-day shop** — open during market hours too, with the panel force-closing at the bell.
- **Diegetic day cycle** — the global "press E to start next day" prompt is gone; ending a day means walking to the exit sign, starting one means the chart wall (invisible `DayStation` zones, editor-movable). Closes the day-start-zone playtest item.
- **Margin items now stack** (5× + 10× = 15×) via a derived-value recompute instead of a set; the host also mirrors item buys into its inventory record, which fixes remote sell-back.
- **Test suite fully green — 218/218.** The 6 "pre-existing" leverage failures turned out to be stale tests: a default risk cap was silently shrinking full-stake opens. Two new tests pin the risk cap and margin stacking.
- **Play-validated:** Matt streamed a full in-game week that night — day cycle, bell, goal curve all held up, positive feedback from viewers.

**TC stake ladder rework (07-17)**

- Every casino booth now presents the same **4-rung stake ladder**: day goal × 10/25/50/75%, times a per-booth scalar derived from max payout multiples so a top-rung win pays ≤ ~1.6 goals anywhere (binary 1.0, prediction 0.5, rug pull 0.4, EOD 0.15). Anchored to `target_for_day`, so stakes ride the goal curve automatically.
- The bottom rung **shrinks to the player's remaining cash** — you can ride the casino to zero.
- New **"High Roller" shop buff** (+25% stakes per purchase) through the standard buff pipeline.
- **Scratch cards rebalanced** (the LAN-V2 "too generous" item): 4 day-scaled ticket tiers; jackpot roll + pity only on full-price-or-better tickets (kills penny-ticket pot farming); jackpot day bonus 1% → 0.2%/day, odds capped at 5%.
- Host validates affordability + top-rung cap; suite now **246/246** including a 24-assert ladder test.

**Market music + wall banners (07-20/21)**

- **Per-market music beds** — each market names a wav and a GameManager-mounted player hits full volume on the opening bell (no fade-in, deliberate) and fades out at close. Gotcha solved after two false trails: a wav imported without its loop flag has `loop_end=0`, so forcing loop mode loops frame-0→frame-0 and plays silence — the loop region now gets set to the full sample.
- **Wall banners** — 8 baked banner slots around the floor now pull per-day adverts from a drop-in texture folder, addressed by material name with per-instance overrides. A seeded shuffle keeps all MP peers in lockstep with zero RPCs. Headless-probed against the real level: 8/8 slots bind. Pool starts empty until real 3:1 adverts are authored.

**Priority reframe: Alpha 2 is the horizon**

No LAN event for a couple of months (~Sept), so the 2-client LAN smoke stops being an urgent gate — MP validation waits for Alpha 2 prep. Focus until then: in-engine maturity and robustness. Runway: remaining design items (trade-sizing workshop, value-vs-shares toggle, EoD confirm + flat test market, commission ramp), content (event/fluff lines toward ~150, banner art, music beds), the MC-12 cohesive style pass, and balance feel passes on the new stakes + goal curve.
