Steamworks configuration, 3D modelling, shader work, and planning this round.

- **Toon shader pipeline** — Cel shading via custom `light()` function (step/mix) applied at runtime by `toon_applicator.gd` to all imported .glb models, skipping transparent materials. Outlines tested and dropped in favour of flat shading only.
- **3D modelling & Blender workflow** — Trading floor modelled and exported as separate .glb meshes (walls, floor, ceiling, skylight). Key lesson learned: each export must use "Selected Only" to avoid duplicate geometry from the full Blender scene.
- **Occlusion fading** — Camera-to-player raycasts hide walls that block the view. Uses visibility toggle rather than transparency (which doesn't work with ShaderMaterial).
- **Day/night lighting** — Directional light rotates and warms across the trading day. At market close: light drops to zero, ambient goes dark, screen emission cranks up, and glow lights appear near monitors.
- **Steam Playtest** is unlocked once the store page hits "Coming Soon" status, which requires art assets that don't exist yet — header capsule, small capsule, main capsule, hero graphic, page background, library capsule, library hero, and at least 4 screenshots.
