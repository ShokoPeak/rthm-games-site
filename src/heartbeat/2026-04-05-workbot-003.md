Two major steps landed plus a round of UI and camera fixes.

- **Item System Expansion** (Step 35) — Shop completely reworked into a host-authoritative draw system. 5 slots per draw (2 buffs, 2 passive, 1 active) with reroll support. New mechanics: max trade size (starts 20%, buffable to 100%), uncapped margin buff, dynamic buff pricing. Inventory limited to 3 passive + 1 active slot, expandable via donation box upgrades. Item selling at 50% refund. NPCs now shop from draws and make donations. Full MP sync.
- **Active Items** (Step 36) — ActiveItemSystem with per-agent equip, cooldown tracking, ADS targeting, and effect dispatch. Three weapons shipped:

  - **Airhorn** — radial 8m, 240 tick cooldown, force closes or force enters trades on targets.
  - **Wallet Inspector** — radial 2m, 480 tick cooldown, steals 25% of target's commission.
  - Right-click ADS (forces mouselook), left-click fire, scroll to cycle. HUD shows equipped item + cooldown bar and stun indicator. Full MP RPC sync for all effects.
- **Lobby layout** reworked to side-by-side (player list left, config right).
- **Ball cam** now has pitch tracking with configurable pitch_strength.
- Various bug fixes: NPC trading, ghost mode, cash leak, double-apply prevention, EOD order queue flush.
