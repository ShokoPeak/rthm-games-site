**Order-queue rework — budget slicing**

The tranche schedule is gone. Three iterations in one session (service-throttle → admission gate → final shape):

- Each tick the floor processes up to `per_tick_budget` of order notional, **strict FIFO**: the front order takes `min(remaining, budget_left)` at that tick's price, leftover flows to the next order. Small orders batch; big orders straddle ticks.
- A multi-tick order settles at **VWAP across tick prices → slippage emerges naturally** from the order-book walk per slice.
- **Fill-bar contract:** the HUD animates a tick's slices sequentially (share-weighted sweeps) and snaps the previous batch when the next tick's slices arrive — pre-empts MP updates, never stalls.
- **One order per agent** + a **self-healing client gate** kill the LAN strand bug (stuck holding / frozen "Working…" buttons; the Airhorn escape hatch should no longer be needed).
- Budget is a whole-lobby upgrade: base 10k → L1 20k → +5k per vote.
- Tested via a headless `FakeGM` stub + 9 queue tests, including the exact multi-order VWAP straddle case.

**Bug-day blitz (playtest items 7–14, all closed)**

- **Exits now mirror entries:** closes unwind proportionate shares against the book per tick and settle at slice VWAP, instead of a one-shot dump.
- **Signed slippage metrics** (entry/exit, fill VWAP vs mid at order start, side-normalised) recorded on the trade ledger — UI surfacing TBD.
- **Cancels are queue-only:** once an order starts processing, the ✕ hides and Cancel flips to "Working…".
- **Ghost HUD rows root-caused:** market close wiped the queue without per-order cancel signals, so still-queued orders left rows forever. Fixed with `OrderQueue.clear_all()` + a HUD sweep on `market_closed`.
- **Equity-includes-stake fix:** the goal readout hand-rolled equity and missed `margin_used`, so parked stake displayed as a loss. Now routes through the canonical `Account.get_equity()`.
- **Mid-bar order admission:** leftover slice budget services orders queued mid-bar at the live mid-bar price (one tick's carry, never stacks).
- **Per-item whiff cooldown:** active items that hit nobody get a short cooldown (`ItemDef.whiff_cooldown_ticks`, tunable per item) instead of the full one; MP echoes the authoritative cooldown to the firing client.
- Plus: PnL Goal abbreviation ($364466 → "$364k"), red/gold/green goal-figure colouring, **soft push** (walk into a stationary body, shove at 20% run speed, fully MP-relayed) and **jump** (Space, 0.8 m — with a focus fix so Space can't re-click BUY/SELL).
