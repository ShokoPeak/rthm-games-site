  - Tickers are deliberate jokes — they're meant to spell things and will never be "corrected"
- **Ticker flavour crawl** — `Config/TickerFlavour.gd` → `UI/NewsTicker.gd` consumes headlines sequentially, once each, no wrap. This is deliberate while Matt sizes the list (target ~150 headlines, written by hand). Shuffle-per-`session_seed` planned once the list is full so MP clients share a deterministic order. Current consumption rate: ~1 headline every ~11s of trading.
- **Screen addressing contract codified** — One addressable in-world screen now requires one unique mesh name OR one unique material name. Caught on `prop_desk_monitor_sm4x` (all four faces had been baked into a single `Mat_Screen` primitive — un-addressable). Fix on the Blender side: split per-face materials (`Mat_Screen`, `Mat_Screen2`, `Mat_Screen3`, `Mat_Screen4`). TC booths already comply (`Mat_Screen_Front/Back/Left/Right/Ticker/Chamfer`) so the trade clusters won't hit the wall when real per-face content lands.
- **`World/MonitorScreens.gd`** — new binder for desk-monitor set-dressing. Matches `Mat_Screen` + numeric suffix (carefully skipping the booth `Mat_Screen_*` underscored faces), skins each face with a random emissive colour. No SubViewports — pure ambience.
- **Pre-existing test failures documented** — 6 leverage P&L tests fail on main as a known pre-existing condition. Not regressions. Flagged so future cycles don't chase them.
