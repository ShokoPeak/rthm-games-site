---
title: "Screen Addressing Contract + Ticker Plumbing"
date: 2026-06-15
---

A smaller technical cycle, but the pipeline got a codified contract for how on-screen surfaces get addressed and a new binder for set-dressing monitors.

- **Screen addressing contract codified** — One addressable in-world screen now requires one unique mesh name OR one unique material name. Multi-screen props that share a single material across faces — or merge faces into one primitive — cannot be driven per-screen under the current by-name binder convention.
- **`prop_desk_monitor_sm4x` material split** — Caught a regression: all four screen faces had been baked into a single `Mat_Screen` primitive, so the `Monitor1..4` nodes were un-addressable mesh-less markers. Fixed Blender-side by splitting per-face materials: sm4x → `Mat_Screen` / `Mat_Screen2` / `Mat_Screen3` / `Mat_Screen4`; sm2x → `Mat_Screen` / `Mat_Screen2`. TC booths already comply (`Mat_Screen_Front/Back/Left/Right/Ticker/Chamfer`) so they won't hit the wall when real per-face content lands.
- **`World/MonitorScreens.gd`** — New binder for desk-monitor set-dressing. Matches `Mat_Screen` + numeric suffix only (carefully skips the booth's `Mat_Screen_*` underscored faces) and skins each face with a random emissive colour. No SubViewports — pure ambient lighting.
- **`Config/TickerFlavour.gd` → `UI/NewsTicker.gd`** — Flavour crawl now consumes headlines sequentially, once each, no wrap. Cursor persists across days and freezes at the list end. Consumption rate ≈ 1 headline every 11 s of trading at current `MARQUEE_GAP=90` / `MARQUEE_SPEED=70`. Shuffle-per-`session_seed` is queued for when the list is fuller, so MP clients will share a deterministic order while keeping variety.
- **Pre-existing test failures documented** — Six leverage P&L tests fail on `main` as a known pre-existing condition. Not regressions. Captured so future cycles don't chase them.

**Next:** still NPCs in BlenderFloor, booth interaction wiring (MC-5/6), trading HUD (MC-9). The Trader Tablet UI work and the toon shader / material substitution pass are queued behind those.
